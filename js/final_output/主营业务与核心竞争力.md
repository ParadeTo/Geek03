Ningbo Shanshan Co., Ltd., with its stock code 600884, is a prominent player in the lithium battery materials industry. Here's an overview of the company's main business, core competencies, and industry position as of December 2025:

### Main Business
Ningbo Shanshan Co., Ltd. primarily operates in the production of lithium battery materials, particularly focusing on anode and cathode materials. The company serves both domestic and international markets, including significant markets like China and Taiwan.
